Occasio House

Project Overview

We provided the full scaffold design for Occasio House — part of Harlow Council’s flagship Playhouse Cultural Quarter regeneration, delivered for main contractor Hill. The scheme replaces a demolished 2001 housing block at Playhouse Square with a new arts and cultural quarter, and Node’s scope covered the full-perimeter access scaffold across the multi-block new-build for MK1 Indigo Group’s scaffolding division.

The Design Challenge

The scaffold had to keep pace with a reinforced concrete frame progressing slab-by-slab across two adjacent blocks of differing heights, with a live tower crane operating between them and a fully hoarded town-centre perimeter separating the site from an active public realm. The access design needed to hand safe working platforms up to the following trades — brickwork, cladding and roofing — immediately behind the concrete frame without ever holding up the crane pour cycle.

A full-perimeter access scaffold was designed to each block with lift-by-lift erection sequenced against the slab pour programme. Cantilevered loading bays were integrated into the scaffold at height for direct material handling off the tower crane, and independent external staircase towers were designed to keep site access separate from the crane exclusion zone and the material bays. Gable ends were sheeted with debris netting for containment through the taller lifts, with the sheeting layout coordinated against the ULS wind analysis so the scaffold remained stable through the summer’s open-frame phase before cladding closed the envelope. Loads to ground were carefully distributed given the constrained site footprint and the presence of concrete slabs, capping beams and buried services at ground level.

What Node Delivered

Full scaffold design package for the Occasio House new-build — 2D construction drawings, complete 3D model and structural calculations — issued to MK1 Indigo Group covering the full-perimeter access scaffold across both blocks, cantilevered loading bays at height, independent external staircase towers and the gable-end sheeting arrangement. The design resolved the sequencing interface between Hill’s slab-by-slab concrete frame pour, the following trades behind and the live tower crane operating on the constrained town-centre plot at the heart of Harlow’s Playhouse Cultural Quarter regeneration.
